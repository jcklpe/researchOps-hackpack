# Trial Brief: Routes to First Contact
**Project:** [Finding Community Support](../project-brief.md)
**Research lead:** Example researcher
**Method:** Survey

## Background and Research Goals
Describe the first routes to support reported in a small example dataset and whether respondents knew what would happen after contacting the center.

## Intended Use and Assumptions
Show how response counts can be reported with their limits. An invitation to current visitors would miss people who never reached the center; responses must not be treated as representative of all residents.

## Methodology and Recruitment
The [survey plan](trial-plan-survey.md) illustrates a short questionnaire offered through a reception-desk invitation during a seven-day response window. Six entirely fabricated responses illustrate the export. No questionnaire was actually distributed.

## Timeline and Review
Illustrative stages: team review and pilot, coordinator approval, seven-day response window, export, then descriptive reporting. A real trial would record calendar dates and the reviewed questionnaire version.
