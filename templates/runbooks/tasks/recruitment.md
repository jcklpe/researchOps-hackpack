# Recruitment and Collection Preparation
**Owner:** the person coordinating participants or survey distribution, with the researcher deciding selection criteria.

## Instructions and Completion Criteria
- [ ] Confirm the reviewed trial brief and plan, intended participants, selection criteria, access needs, and recruitment approach with the researcher.
- [ ] Prepare invitations and a recruitment record in appropriate storage; link the record for people who need access. Keep contact details out of public task descriptions.
- [ ] For interviews or user tests: arrange the selected sessions, facilitators, observers, materials, and participant communications. Address expected cancellations in the trial's own plan rather than assuming a universal over-recruitment rate.
- [ ] For surveys: prepare the distribution channel, invitation, tested questionnaire link, opening/closing dates, and any planned reminders. Individual appointments are not required.
- [ ] Confirm the project's participation/consent arrangements and any compensation or follow-up responsibilities.
- [ ] Hand readiness and outstanding issues to the collection owner. Record recruitment shortfalls and changes so they can be considered in reporting.
