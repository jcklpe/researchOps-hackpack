# Research Project Grouping / Epic
Use an epic, parent task, board grouping, or equivalent in the team's tracker.

## Context
Record the project name, brief location, research lead, and relevant collaborators. Link the project brief and each trial's tasks here as they are created.

## Completion Criteria
- [ ] The project's purpose, scope, and document location are visible to its team.
- [ ] Related work is grouped and has owners; review responsibilities are clear.
- [ ] At project completion, agreed trial outputs and unresolved follow-up work are linked and the research lead has confirmed the handoff.
