# Synthesize and Report
**Owner:** researcher. **Reviewers:** the team's chosen reviewers and relevant stakeholders.

## Instructions and Completion Criteria
- [ ] Bring together the trial's artifacts and relevant debriefs. Preserve the route to originals and record significant transformations or analysis choices.
- [ ] Analyze the material using an approach appropriate to the inquiry. Separate observations from interpretations and unsupported possibilities.
- [ ] Write the trial report, linking findings to evidence and stating participation, denominators where relevant, limitations, and unanswered questions.
- [ ] Connect recommendations to findings and identify their intended audience or owner.
- [ ] Share with the team for feedback, return revisions to the researcher, and record the resulting changes.
- [ ] Arrange the agreed stakeholder presentation or review, resolve required revisions, and record the handoff or sign-off.
- [ ] Link the report and follow-up work from the trial tasks and project grouping. Mark the work complete in the chosen tracker when the agreed handoff is complete.

**Document template:** `templates/trial-report.md` from the toolkit root. Existing `synthesis-methods.md` provides additional ideas, not an automatic choice of method.
