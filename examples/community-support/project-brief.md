# Project Brief: Finding Community Support
**Fictional worked example.** The organization, participants, activities, and results in this project are invented to demonstrate the toolkit. The three trials illustrate choices, not a required sequence.

**Collaborators and reviewers:** Example service coordinator and support team

## Background
An imaginary community center offers advice, food support, and referrals through a website, telephone line, and reception desk. The team wants to understand how residents work out where to begin, including when they do not know the name of a service.

## Research Goals
- Understand how people describe their need and choose an initial route to support.
- Identify information that helps them decide whether to take the next step.

### Intended Use
Help the team revise its service information and consider changes across its website and reception process. This inquiry concerns several services rather than a single product team.

### Assumptions and Hypotheses
The team suspects that service names may be less useful than descriptions of the help offered. That is an assumption to examine, not an established finding.
