# Collect Research Material
**Owner:** the researcher or designated collection lead. **Collaborators:** facilitators, observers, or survey distribution support as needed.

## Instructions and Completion Criteria
- [ ] Confirm the reviewed plan, recruitment readiness, access to materials, and the project's participation/consent arrangements.
- [ ] For interviews or user tests: conduct the planned sessions. Keep identifiable observer notes and associated artifacts together by session. Record departures from the plan and hold useful observer debriefs.
- [ ] For surveys: distribute the reviewed questionnaire, monitor the response window and access problems, carry out agreed reminders, then close collection. Retain the source export and its questionnaire version, window, and exclusions/missing-data context.
- [ ] Update the recruitment or collection record to reflect what actually happened.
- [ ] Check that the researcher can retrieve the collected materials from the linked storage locations and understand their source and authorship.
- [ ] Hand the material, limitations, and relevant debriefs to the synthesis/report owner.

**Artifact guidance:** `guides/research-artifacts.md` from the toolkit root. Collection work and substantive research remain human responsibilities in this initial agent-assisted toolkit.
