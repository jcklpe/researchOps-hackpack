# Trial Brief: Finding the Next Step
**Project:** [Finding Community Support](../project-brief.md)

## Background and Research Goals
Observe whether a person can find how to ask about food support on an imaginary service-information page, and what interrupts their attempt.

## Intended Use and Assumptions
Inform the placement and wording of next-step information. We suspect the page's labels may obscure the route, but an observed difficulty must not automatically be attributed to its wording.

## Methodology and Recruitment
The [user-testing plan](trial-plan-user-testing.md) supplies a task and describes the imaginary page. This example contains one simulated session. A real trial would recruit people relevant to the service and provide an actual testable version.
