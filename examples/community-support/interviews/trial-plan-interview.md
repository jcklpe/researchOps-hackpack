# Trial Plan: Interview
**Trial:** [Trial brief](trial-brief.md)
**Duration:** 20 minutes in this fictional example

## Intro
Explain that we want to understand an experience of looking for support, that there are no preferred answers, and that the participant can skip questions or stop. Introduce the observer. In this example we take notes without recording; a real project must agree its own arrangements.

## Warm-up
Tell me a little about the situation in which you were looking for help. Please share only what you are comfortable discussing.

## Main Questions
- Think of the most recent time you tried to find community support. Where did you start?
- What information did you see or hear, and what did you do next?
- Was there a point when you were unsure what to do? Walk me through it.
- What helped you decide to contact someone?

## Closing
Invite any final comments, thank the participant, and explain how to contact the research team. These sample questions illustrate a filled plan; agents must not copy them as a real researcher's inquiry.
