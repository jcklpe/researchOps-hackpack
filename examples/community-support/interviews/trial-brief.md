## Background and Research Goals
Explore how a resident decided whom to contact when seeking community support, and what made the next step clear or uncertain.

## Intended Use and Assumptions
Inform descriptions of routes to help. We suspect that people may look for plain descriptions before organizational service names. One account can suggest questions for further work; it cannot establish a general pattern.
